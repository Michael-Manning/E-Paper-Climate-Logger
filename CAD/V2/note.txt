I wanted the case to have rounded corners, so I made this version in FreeCAD.

The model works well, but the parameters are a mess. I just copied over all the variables from the OpenSCAD model and didn't even use half of them.

The other big change is that this version uses 5mm M2 screws without a heat insert. It just has self tap holes to make assembly easier.
It also has a small retention clip on the corner furthest from a screw to help keep it flat.

Notes for putting it together with this case:
- don't use/desolder the battery connector. This case only fits if you solder the battery directly to the PCB.
- Tuck the excess battery wire into the side of the battery compartment.
- Print with a 0.2mm nozzle and 0.08mm layer height.
- Print in PLA and use PETG for the support interface layer on the back for smoother finish.
- Sand the edges and corners of the PCB if the fit is too tight.
- Do not force anything. It should come together easily and the case will bend if you force it together.
